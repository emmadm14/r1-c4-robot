# Project Cost Assessment

At the end of the first development year, a complete cost assessment was carried out to estimate the overall value of the R1-C4 project.

The analysis considered both the material cost of the prototype and the engineering time dedicated to its design, development, testing and documentation.

## Costing Method

The assessment was divided into two main categories:

- **Material cost**: based on the estimated purchase price and quantity of the components and materials required for the prototype.
- **Engineering cost**: based on the number of hours dedicated to the project.

An hourly engineering cost of **€23.75** was used for the calculation.

## Estimated Cost

The resulting estimates were:

- **Material cost:** €849.90
- **Engineering cost:** €9,690.00
- **Estimated total project cost:** €10,539.90

The engineering time included:

- **Emma:** 200 hours
- **Vladimir:** 208 hours

## Cost Distribution

The material assessment included the main components required for the prototype, such as:

- Embedded electronics and control components
- Motors and motor drivers
- Mechanical transmission components
- Linear actuator and bearings
- Structural materials
- 3D-printing material
- Fasteners and assembly hardware

## Purpose of the Assessment

This assessment provided a broader view of the R1-C4 project by taking into account not only the purchase price of the physical components, but also the engineering time required to develop the prototype.

It highlights the importance of design, prototyping, integration, testing and documentation in the overall cost of an engineering project.

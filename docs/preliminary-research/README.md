# Preliminary Research

Before starting the design of R1-C4, a preliminary research phase was carried out to study existing robotic solutions and define the most suitable architecture for the project.

The objective was to avoid starting the mechanical design without first analysing the main technical constraints and comparing several possible approaches.

The research focused on the main subsystems of the robot:

- Wheel and drive systems
- 2-3-2 transformation mechanism
- Central retractable foot
- Rotating head/turret
- Motorisation and actuator selection
- Mechanical stability and integration constraints

Several existing R2-D2-inspired robots, prototypes and mechanisms were analysed in order to understand their advantages, limitations and suitability for a robot built at approximately 3/5 scale.

This preliminary study helped guide the first design choices and provided a technical basis for the initial development of R1-C4.

## Main Design Considerations

The research phase highlighted several key requirements:

- The robot had to remain stable during the transition between two-wheel and three-wheel configurations.
- The 2-3-2 mechanism had to control the inclination of the body within a defined angle.
- The central foot had to move vertically while remaining aligned and stable.
- The wheel system had to provide sufficient torque while remaining compact.
- The head had to rotate independently from the body.
- The overall architecture had to remain modular and accessible for maintenance.

The conclusions from this research were then used to define the first version of the mechanical architecture and select the main components for the prototype.
